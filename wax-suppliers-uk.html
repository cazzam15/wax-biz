Wholesale wax suppliers and manufacturers

Buying direct from a manufacturer

Kerax, founded in 1962 and based in Chorley, Lancashire, manufactures soy, coconut, olive, rapeseed, paraffin and hybrid waxes. It also sells EcoSoya and EcoCoco grades. Its product pages state a minimum total order of six 20kg boxes, and you can mix products to reach it. Delivery is priced by the number of pallets and your postcode. On 28 October 2026, EcoSoya Melt was listed at £125.00 for 20kg (£6.25/kg) on kerax.com. That is higher than some retailers' 20kg prices for the same wax, so buying direct isn't automatically cheaper at small volumes. Kerax lists price breaks from 240kg upwards.

  • Craftovator sells most waxes in 1kg, 5kg, 10kg and 20kg sizes and has a Wholesale page. Its banner said an annual stock check was under way on 28 October.
  • Livemoor sells soy waxes from 100g up to 20kg and has a Wholesale/Trade Orders page. The site now also sells garden and home products.
  • The Soapery says it has a bulk sales team for business-to-business orders.
  • Cosy Owl says "trade accounts welcome" and offers free delivery over £250 inc VAT.
  • The Soap Kitchen mainly supplies soap and cosmetic makers. Check its site for wax and trade terms.

Wax distributors: what the word means

A distributor buys wax in large volumes from manufacturers such as Kerax, AAK (Golden Wax) or NatureWax, then sells it in smaller bags. Most of the shops in the table work this way. That's why the same grade, such as EcoSoya Melt, appears at several suppliers at different prices. When you compare, match the exact grade and bag size, then add delivery.

When to go wholesale

Retail bags (500g to 1kg) suit testing a new wax. Bigger bags cost less per kg. Using real prices checked on 28 October 2026, Candle Shack's Nature Wax C-3 cost £5.43/kg in an 800g bag, £3.88/kg in a 5kg bag and £3.54/kg in a 22.68kg box. FizzyWhiz's EcoSoya Melt cost £9.95/kg at 1kg, £6.59/kg at 5kg and £5.25/kg at 20kg.

Wholesale usually makes sense when:

  • you make melts every week and use 20kg within a few months
  • you've settled on your recipe and no longer need to test lots of waxes
  • you have dry, cool space to store a 20kg box
  • you've added delivery to the price, because some free-delivery offers exclude wax or heavy boxes

Safety paperwork: SDS and CLP

In Great Britain, the classification and labelling of hazardous chemical mixtures falls under the GB CLP Regulation, which the Health and Safety Executive oversees. See HSE: The GB CLP Regulation. Your fragrance supplier's Safety Data Sheet (SDS) gives you the hazard information you need to work out your label. Use our CLP label checklist.

Supplier questions

Who are the main UK wax suppliers for wax melts?

The suppliers compared here include Candle Shack, Craftovator, Livemoor, The Soapery, FizzyWhiz, Craftastik, Blossom Oils, The Oil Store UK, Cosy Owl and NI Candle Supplies, plus the manufacturer Kerax. We checked each one's own website on 28 October 2026 for wax types, bag sizes, prices and delivery terms.

Which suppliers sell wholesale or bulk, and what is the minimum order?

Most retailers here sell wax in 20kg to 25kg boxes with no stated minimum order. Kerax, the manufacturer, states a minimum total order of six 20kg boxes. The Soapery has a business-to-business bulk team and Cosy Owl offers trade accounts.

Where can I buy wax direct from a manufacturer?

Kerax, based in Chorley, Lancashire, manufactures wax and sells direct in 20kg boxes up to 900kg pallets, with delivery priced by pallet and postcode. Many UK retailers stock Kerax and EcoSoya waxes in smaller bags.

Which suppliers give SDS, CLP and IFRA data with fragrance oils?

Fragrance Oils Direct says all its oils have allergen reports, IFRA statements, SDS and CLP information. Craftovator's fragrance pages link CLP, SDS and IFRA documents. For any other supplier, ask for the Safety Data Sheet before you buy, because you need it to label your melts.

When is bulk buying worth it?

Bulk makes sense once your recipe is settled, you make melts every week and you have dry storage for a 20kg box. On 28 October 2026, Candle Shack's Nature Wax C-3 cost £5.43 per kg in an 800g bag and £3.54 per kg in a 22.68kg box.

Is Amazon OK for wax melt supplies?

Amazon is handy for small top-ups, equipment and packaging, but check the seller, the exact wax grade and whether an SDS is available. Dedicated suppliers usually give more technical data. Our Amazon picks are affiliate links.
