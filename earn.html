Five Ways to Earn

You don't need to sell products to make money from a directory site. Here are five income streams that work specifically for The Wax Edit, ordered from easiest to set up to most lucrative over time.

Active now 📦

Amazon Associates

Potential: £30–£300/month

Already active on your site. Every click that leads to a purchase earns you 1–10% commission depending on the product category. Candle making supplies, books and home & garden products all earn well.

  • Drive traffic to your Amazon Picks page via social media
  • Add seasonal product roundups (Christmas gifts, winter warmers)
  • Write blog posts that naturally link to Amazon products
  • Make 3 qualifying sales within 180 days to keep account active
Effort: Low · Already set up Amazon Associates →
Easy to add 🔗

Retailer Affiliate Programmes

Potential: £50–£500/month

Many of the UK retailers already listed on your site run their own affiliate programmes. When a visitor clicks your affiliate link and makes a purchase, you earn a percentage — often 5–15%, much higher than Amazon.

  • Sign up to Craftovator, Candle Shack and The Soap Kitchen affiliate schemes
  • Replace standard links with your affiliate tracking links
  • Most programmes are free to join via Awin or Rakuten networks
  • Add "affiliate link" disclosure text on retailer pages
Effort: Low · 1–2 hours to set up Join Awin Network →
Easy to add 💷

Display Advertising

Potential: £20–£200/month

Once your site has traffic, you can add display ads that pay per impression or click. Google AdSense is the easiest to start — you apply, get approved, paste a code snippet and ads appear automatically.

  • Apply for Google AdSense at google.com/adsense (needs ~100 visitors/month)
  • Paste the AdSense script into your HTML pages
  • For higher rates once traffic grows, switch to Mediavine (50k sessions/month) or Ezoic
  • Keep ads tasteful — sidebar or footer placement works best for directories
Effort: Low · Needs traffic first Google AdSense →
Build over time 📧

Email Newsletter & List

Potential: £100–£1,000+/month

An email list is the most valuable long-term asset for a content site. A newsletter lets you promote affiliate products directly to subscribers, sell sponsored spots, and build a loyal audience that returns every week.

  • Add a free email signup form (Mailchimp or Kit — both free to start)
  • Offer a freebie to drive signups — e.g. "Free UK Wax Melt Supplier Checklist"
  • Send a weekly or fortnightly roundup with tips, new products and affiliate links
  • Sponsored newsletter spots can be sold to retailers for £50–£200 per send
Effort: Medium · Consistent weekly effort Start with Mailchimp →
Longer term 📋

Digital Products

Potential: £200–£2,000+/month

The highest-margin income stream. Sell downloadable resources directly to your audience — once created, they sell repeatedly with no extra work. Your guides and regulatory knowledge make you credible to sell premium resources.

  • CLP label template pack (PDF) — sell for £5–15
  • Wax melt recipe & batch tracking spreadsheet — sell for £5–10
  • UK craft fair pricing calculator — sell for £5–12
  • Sell via Etsy, Gumroad or Ko-fi — all take small % per sale
  • "Complete Guide to Selling Wax Melts in the UK" ebook — sell for £12–25
Effort: High upfront · Passive income after Sell on Gumroad →
Grow audience first 📱

Social Media & Sponsorship

Potential: £100–£1,000+/month

A TikTok, Instagram or YouTube channel covering UK wax melt making tips, supplier reviews and beginner guides drives traffic to your site and opens up brand partnerships and sponsorship deals with UK suppliers.

  • Short-form video content works best — supplier hauls, fragrance reviews, how-tos
  • Link your website in bio to drive affiliate clicks
  • Once you have 5k+ followers, approach smaller UK suppliers for paid partnerships
  • TikTok Shop affiliate lets you earn on products shown in videos
Effort: High · Best long-term multiplier TikTok for Business →

Your Income Roadmap

A realistic timeline for building income from The Wax Edit — based on consistent effort and steady traffic growth.

Month 1–2 — Right Now
Activate affiliate revenue

Your Amazon affiliate is live. Sign up to 2–3 retailer affiliate programmes via Awin and replace their directory links with tracked versions. Share your site on Facebook craft groups, Reddit (r/candlemaking) and any maker communities you're in. Target: first affiliate clicks.

Month 2–4
Build traffic & start your email list

Add a Mailchimp signup form offering a free PDF (supplier checklist, recipe tracker). Post consistently on one social channel. Apply for Google AdSense once you have steady visitors. Target: 500+ monthly visitors, 50+ email subscribers.

Month 4–8
Create your first digital product

Use your regulatory knowledge to create a CLP label template pack or UK craft pricing calculator. List it on Gumroad or Etsy for £5–15. Promote to your email list and social following. Target: first product sales, £50–200/month total income.

Month 8–12
Scale what's working

Double down on whichever channel is driving the most traffic. If social is working, post more. If organic search is growing, write more guides. Approach 1–2 UK suppliers about a sponsored newsletter slot or review partnership. Target: £200–500/month.

Year 2+
Multiple income streams running together

Amazon + retailer affiliates + AdSense + email newsletter sponsorships + digital products all running simultaneously. At 10,000+ monthly visitors this is a genuinely significant side income. The Wax Edit becomes the go-to UK resource for craft makers.

01
Disclose affiliates clearly

UK law (CAP Code) requires you to clearly disclose when links are affiliate links. Add "This page contains affiliate links" at the top of relevant pages. Already done on your Amazon page.

02
Traffic first, monetise second

The more visitors you have, the more everything earns. Focus on building genuine useful content and driving traffic before worrying about maximising rates. Useful content attracts links and shares.

03
SEO is free traffic forever

Your buying guides are exactly the content that ranks on Google. Add more guides ("best wax for beginners", "how to start selling wax melts UK") and traffic compounds over time with no ad spend.

04
Test and track everything

Add Google Analytics (free) to see which pages get traffic and which Amazon links get clicked. Double down on what's working. Your data will tell you which income stream to prioritise.
