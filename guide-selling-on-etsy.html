Selling Wax Melts on Etsy

What Etsy really takes from each sale, what your listing has to show under UK CLP rules, and what changes when buyers in the EU or Northern Ireland order from you.

Home / Buying Guides / Selling on Etsy

Etsy is where many UK wax melt makers make their first online sale. It is quick to set up, but the fees add up, and a wax melt listing has to carry the same safety information as the label on your clamshell. This guide covers the costs and the rules, with figures taken from Etsy's own help pages on 23 November 2026.

Etsy's fees for UK sellers

These are the fees most wax melt sellers pay, for a shop paid into a UK bank account. Etsy works out the percentage fees on the whole order, so the postage you charge is included.

FeeHow muchWhen you pay it
Listing fee$0.20 USD per listingWhen you publish a listing, whether it sells or not. Also charged again when an auto-renewed listing sells, and for each extra item in a multi-quantity order
Transaction fee6.5% of the order totalWhen an item sells. Includes postage and gift wrap
Payment processing4% + £0.20On each order paid through Etsy Payments, including postage
Regulatory Operating fee0.48%On the item price plus postage, for UK sellers
Offsite Ads15% (12% for bigger shops)Only on orders that came from an Etsy ad on another site

Etsy adds VAT to its seller fees, including the processing fee, and you can only claim that back if you are VAT registered. Sources: Etsy fee basics, payment processing fees, Regulatory Operating fee and Offsite Ads.

A worked example

Say a customer buys one pack of melts at £4.50 and you charge £3.00 postage, so the order is £7.50.

  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of £7.50 = about £0.49
  • Payment processing: 4% of £7.50 (£0.30) + £0.20 = £0.50
  • Regulatory Operating fee: 0.48% of £7.50 = about £0.04
  • VAT on those fees brings the total to about £1.23

That is about a sixth of the order gone before the $0.20 listing fee, your wax, oil, clamshell, label and the real cost of postage. Small single-pack orders are hit hardest by the fixed £0.20, which is one reason many makers sell bundles. Put your own numbers into the free pricing calculator, which has a setting for Etsy fees.

Offsite Ads: know the rule

Etsy enrols every shop in Offsite Ads automatically. If your shop has always made less than $10,000 USD in any 365 days, you can opt out, and the fee is 15% on orders that came from an ad. Once a shop has made $10,000 USD or more in any 365 days, the fee drops to 12% but the shop must stay in for good. On a low-priced product like wax melts, 15% can wipe out your profit on that order, so decide early whether to opt out, and build it into your prices if you stay in.

What your listing must show

Most scented wax melts need a CLP label, and that rule follows you online. Article 48 of GB CLP says any advert for a hazardous mixture that lets the public buy without first seeing the label "shall mention the type or types of hazard indicated on the label". An Etsy listing is exactly that kind of advert.

  • Add a clear, readable photo of the CLP label for each scent.
  • Write the hazard and precautionary statements, and any allergens named on the label, in the description, so buyers can read them without zooming in.
  • Keep your supplier's safety data sheet (SDS) for every fragrance oil. Your label is built from it, so check the label still matches whenever a supplier sends a new SDS.
  • Make sure the scent, weight and fragrance load in the listing match the batch you actually post.

Not sure what goes on the label? Run through our free CLP label checklist, read 10% or 25% CLP? if your oil's SDS gives two sets of figures, and see the testing & safety guide for the wider rules.

Buyers in the EU and Northern Ireland

Etsy treats EU countries, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Northern Ireland as "GPSR countries", under the EU's General Product Safety Regulation. Etsy's GPSR help page says that if you sell physical products to buyers there as a trader, you need to:

  • add an EU-based economic operator and/or the manufacturer's details to your listings (a maker based outside the EU can appoint an EU Responsible Person, and Etsy points to partner companies that offer this), and
  • fill in the Product safety info field with your warnings and instructions.

If that is more than you want to take on, you can opt out: in Shop Manager, go to Settings, then Options, and choose not to sell physical items to GPSR countries. Remember that Northern Ireland is on that list, and that NI follows EU CLP, where a UFI code and poison centre notification are required. Many small makers start with Great Britain only and add Europe later.

Listings that sell

  • Say what's in the pack. Number of pieces or bars, total weight in grams, wax type and the scent notes.
  • Show the size. A photo with a hand, a warmer or a coin next to the melts saves a lot of questions.
  • Explain how to use them. Which warmers they suit, how many pieces to use, and to never leave a warmer unattended.
  • Use the words buyers search for. Scent names, "soy wax melts", "snap bar" and gift ideas work better than clever product names.

Posting wax melts

Wax melts are light, which keeps postage low, but they chip and soften. Pack clamshells snugly so they can't rattle, use a stiff box or padded mailer, and take care in hot weather, when melts can soften in a van. Weigh a packed order before you set your Etsy postage price, so you don't lose money on every sale. The packaging guide covers clamshells and boxes.

Common questions

How much does Etsy take from a wax melt sale in the UK?

For a seller with a UK bank account, Etsy charges a 6.5% transaction fee, a 4% + £0.20 payment processing fee and a 0.48% Regulatory Operating fee, all worked out on the order including postage. There is also a $0.20 USD listing fee, and VAT is added to the fees. On a £7.50 order that comes to about £1.23 before the listing fee.

Does my Etsy listing need to show CLP information?

Yes, if your wax melt is classified as hazardous. GB CLP Article 48 says an advert that lets the public buy without first seeing the label must mention the type or types of hazard shown on the label. Put a clear photo of your CLP label in the listing and write the hazard statements in the description.

Can I stop buyers in the EU and Northern Ireland ordering?

Yes. Etsy lets you opt out of selling physical items to GPSR countries in Shop Manager under Settings, then Options. If you do want to sell there, Etsy asks you to add an EU-based economic operator and product safety information to your listings.

Do I have to use Offsite Ads?

Not at first. Etsy automatically enrols every shop, but if your shop has always made under $10,000 USD in any 365 days you can opt out, and you only pay the 15% fee on orders that came from an ad. Once a shop reaches $10,000 USD it pays 12% and can't opt out.
